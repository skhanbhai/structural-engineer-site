What does a stepped crack in brickwork mean?

Short answer
One part of the wall has moved relative to the part next to it and the mortar gave way before the bricks did. The shape alone says nothing about how serious the movement was.

Is it normal for new plaster to crack?

Short answer
Usually, yes. Fresh plaster shrinks as it dries and hairline cracks follow. A few patterns mean the wall behind has moved.
Read the guide →

22 Aug 202610 min read

Is it subsidence or just settlement?

Short answer
Settlement is a building bedding down and it stops. Subsidence is the ground moving and it does not. The taper of the crack helps tell them apart.
Read the guide →

22 Aug 20269 min read

Does home insurance cover cracked walls?

Short answer
It depends on the cause. Subsidence, heave and impact are normally covered. Shrinkage, thermal movement and wear are not.

How these guides are written.

One question each

A guide answers the question in its title and nothing else. The answer comes first and the reasoning follows for anyone who wants it.

By an engineer, not a copywriter

Every guide is written by a chartered structural engineer and describes what we look for when we stand in front of the wall.

Honest about what is normal

Most of the cracks we are sent turn out to be cosmetic. The guides say so and they say which signs mean it is time to ask.

Drawn, not stock

The diagrams are drawn for the guide they sit in. They show the pattern you are trying to match against your own wall.
